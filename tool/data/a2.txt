Binturong|Arctictis binturong|Binturong|Forests of South and Southeast Asia|It is also called a bearcat but is neither a bear nor a cat.;;It smells like buttered popcorn due to a compound in its urine.;;It has a prehensile tail, unusual among carnivores of the Old World.;;It helps spread seeds of strangler figs.

Aardvark|Orycteropus afer|Aardvark|Sub-Saharan Africa|Its name means 'earth pig' in Afrikaans.;;It can dig burrows incredibly fast with its powerful claws.;;It has teeth with no enamel that keep growing.;;Its abandoned burrows provide shelter for many other animals.
